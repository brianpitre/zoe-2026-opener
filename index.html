In the Beginning

A suspended field of light that performs John 1 — live, over the whole room.
scroll to begin ⌄
The big idea

The lights aren't decoration.
They're the light of John 1.

This year's theme lives in one sentence — "In him was life, and that life was the light of all mankind. The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." Your opener voiceover is already a journey from a dark void into overwhelming light. So rather than point lights at that idea, we hang the idea over the room: roughly 1,200 of the leftover xylobands, suspended on fishing line above the stage and the audience, choreographed to perform the script in real time — a field that begins as nothing, becomes a starfield, and ends as a blaze the whole room is standing inside.

"In the beginning was the Word." — John 1:1

The shape of it

One song, three acts of light.

i
The song

Indiana Bible College's John 1 opens with strong vocals. The ceiling stays dark — no one knows it's there yet.

ii
The voiceover

Room to black, then one light. The field is reborn as the music builds underneath.

iii
The worship

The music crests, the field blazes, and the team leads the room into worship.

One · the before

The void

"Before the first heartbeat of time… there was the Word."

Blackout. Then a single, steady light — the first thing the room sees, suspended in the dark with the faintest pool spilling onto the stage.

Two · creation

The starfield

"Through Him all things were made."

One light multiplies into a canopy of cool-white stars, then snaps to a single unified shimmer on "galaxies align."

Three · life

The wildfire

"And in Him is Life… a wildfire."

The whole field breaks to warm gold and surges — life igniting across the room.

Four · the turn

The night

"The enemy whispers that the night is winning. But the Word has already spoken."

The field collapses toward darkness, then stabs back — a burst on every verb: shines, pierces, cuts, shatters.

Five · the light

The Light is here

"The Beginning is here. The Light is here. Jesus is here."

Full blaze. ~1,200 points at once as worship lands — the room isn't watching the light anymore, it's standing inside it.

How it works

Hung over the room, run like an instrument.

  • ≈1,200 of the ~2,000 leftover xylobands, on clear fishing line at staggered heights.
  • Over the stage and the front of house — so the audience ends up inside the field.
  • Driven by the band control system, choreographed to the voiceover and music.
Staying on brand

Built from your Zoe 2026 world.

Warm gold for the light. Deep burgundy and sage lifted from the save-the-date. The same golden-hour, illuminated-manuscript feel — so the opener looks like it grew out of the brand, not bolted on.
